Privacy Policy & Security

App: Griddly (com.megazone.griddly)  •  Developer: MegaZone  •  Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Griddly collects, stores, and shares when you play our nonogram logic puzzle mobile application, use optional cloud sync or community features, make an in-app purchase, or view advertisements.

By using Griddly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Core gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device so you can play offline:

  • Gameplay & Progress: Unlocked levels, stars, hints, flawless solve counts, daily puzzle completion history, and streaks.
  • Preferences & Safety Controls: Theme mode, sound, ambient audio, haptics, gameplay assists, Ad-Free purchase flag, and locally blocked players/creators.

3. Online Leaderboards & Community Workshop (Supabase)

Griddly uses Supabase to power Global Standings and the Community Workshop without requiring an email address, phone number, or password:

  • Anonymous Session Identifier: An anonymous, random device user ID (UUID) is generated to associate your scores and published puzzles.
  • Public Player Profile: Your chosen display name, optional country/region badge, optional preset icon or user-uploaded compressed JPEG profile photo, and puzzle statistics are visible to other players on the Global Leaderboard.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Custom puzzles, puzzle titles, author handles, likes, and moderation reports you submit are stored in our database. Offensive content can be reported or blocked directly inside the app.
You can edit your display name, switch back to a built-in badge, or clear your uploaded profile photo at any time from the Profile dialog in Settings or Leaderboard.

4. Optional Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services, Griddly accesses your Play Games player profile name/ID and uses Google Play Games cloud features to back up and restore your campaign progress, stars, and daily streak across devices. Signing in is optional and can be disconnected at any time in Settings.

5. Advertising & Consent Choices (Google AdMob)

Unless you purchase the one-time Remove Ads upgrade, Griddly displays banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between completed levels, and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • Information Used by AdMob: Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP-derived coarse location, device characteristics, diagnostics, and ad interaction events for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement.
  • Consent Management (EEA, UK & Switzerland): Griddly integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request regional privacy consent before serving ads where required. You can update or withdraw your consent choices anytime via Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices (when applicable) or your Android device ad settings.
  • Ad-Free Upgrade: When Remove Ads is active, all banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads are permanently disabled.

Learn how Google handles partner app data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

6. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Griddly receives purchase verification tokens and entitlement status from Google Play and does not collect, process, or store your payment card or bank information.

7. Security, Retention & Data Deletion

  • Transport & Access Security: Network requests to Supabase and Google services use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, and database access is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Retention & Deletion: Local app data can be cleared by resetting progress in Settings or uninstalling Griddly. To request deletion of your anonymous Supabase leaderboard profile, uploaded avatar photo, or published community puzzles, email us with your in-game player handle and region.

8. Age Requirement & Children's Privacy

Griddly is intended for general audiences (ages 13 and older, or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through public profile fields or community puzzles, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Griddly evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

For privacy inquiries, content takedowns, or data deletion requests, contact:
Email: support@megazone.com

10. Community Guidelines

By using Griddly and participating in public sharing of puzzles, names, or photos, you agree that your content follows family-safe Community Guidelines. Abusive, inappropriate, or harmful content may be reported and removed at our discretion.
